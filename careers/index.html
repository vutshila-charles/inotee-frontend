Domiciliary Care Assistant

Belfast, Lisburn, Bangor, and surrounding areas

Who the role suits: People who are kind, dependable, and comfortable supporting adults in their own homes.

Typical duties: Personal care, medication prompts, meal support, companionship, and helping people stay safe at home.

Requirements: Care experience is helpful. A driving licence may be preferred for community roles.

Start application

Live-In Caregiver

Northern Ireland

Who the role suits: Carers who build trust well and can support someone consistently in their home environment.

Typical duties: Daily support, companionship, practical household help, and close communication with families.

Requirements: Experience with longer-form support and a calm, reliable working style are helpful.

Start application

Night Support Worker

Belfast and surrounding areas

Who the role suits: People who are calm, observant, and comfortable supporting safety and reassurance overnight.

Typical duties: Night checks, mobility support, reassurance, and responding to care needs during vulnerable hours.

Requirements: Night shift availability and care experience are helpful.

Start application

Support Worker

Northern Ireland

Who the role suits: People who want to support adults with practical day-to-day help at home and in the community.

Typical duties: Routine support, appointments, companionship, and encouragement with independence.

Requirements: Good communication, reliability, and a respectful approach to person-centred support.
